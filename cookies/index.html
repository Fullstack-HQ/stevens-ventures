Cookies Policy

Last updated: 3 September 2026

The short version

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. It sets no first-party tracking cookies of its own.

What is actually set

The site is delivered through Cloudflare, which may set strictly necessary cookies used for security and to keep the service working — for example, to identify trusted traffic and mitigate malicious requests. These are not used to build a profile of you or to target advertising.

What is not set

  • No Google Analytics or Google Tag Manager.
  • No Meta, LinkedIn, or other advertising pixels.
  • No session-recording or heatmap tools.
  • No cross-site tracking cookies.

Because this site sets no analytics or advertising cookies, there is no consent banner to accept or decline. If that ever changes, this page and the site will change with it.

Third-party resources

Some fonts and shared code libraries load from Google Fonts, cdnjs and jsDelivr. Those providers receive your IP address in order to deliver the file. They are not used by us to track you across sites.

Managing cookies

Every major browser lets you view, block and delete cookies in its settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may affect whether parts of the site work.
