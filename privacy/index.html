Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 September 2026

The short version

Stevens Ventures does not run analytics, advertising trackers, or third-party marketing pixels on this website. The only personal information we hold is what you type into a form and send us on purpose.

What we collect

Two forms on this site collect information, and both are things you choose to fill in:

  • The contact form asks for your name, your email address, and which area you are interested in. There is an optional message field.
  • The newsletter field in the site footer collects an email address.

We do not ask for, and this site does not collect, payment details, government identifiers, or financial account information.

Information collected automatically

This website is hosted on Netlify and delivered through Cloudflare. Like essentially all web infrastructure, those providers process technical information — including your IP address, browser type, and the pages requested — in order to serve the site and protect it from abuse. Fonts and a small number of shared code libraries are loaded from Google Fonts, cdnjs and jsDelivr, which means those providers also receive your IP address when a page loads.

We do not currently operate Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, a Meta pixel, or any comparable analytics or advertising technology on this site.

How we use it

  • To reply to you and to route your enquiry to the right person.
  • To send the newsletter, if you asked for it.
  • To keep the site running, secure and available.

Who we share it with

We share personal information with service providers who help us operate — for example our hosting, email and content-delivery providers — and only so far as they need it to do that work. We may also disclose information where we are legally required to.

Stevens Ventures does not sell personal information, and does not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as needed to deal with them and to maintain a record of our correspondence, and newsletter subscriptions until you unsubscribe.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected, or to have it deleted. To make any of these requests, contact us using the details below and we will respond as required by applicable law.

Children

This site is intended for business and investment audiences and is not directed to children.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page.
